## Caveats
Product pages change and may be region-specific. SnapCalorie values are vendor reported. Studies differ in datasets and ground truth; none guarantees performance for this app/Indian food. IFCT public access does not settle redistribution rights. No model+dataset pair was cleared for a production Indian food estimator without device and rights validation.
