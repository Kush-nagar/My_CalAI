# Competitor analysis
Features and prices can vary by region, platform, account, and release. Marketing claims are not independent validation.

| Product | Publicly documented strengths | Access snapshot | Limitation |
|---|---|---|---|
| Cal AI | Meal-photo flow; iPhone/Watch listing | Scan results subscription-gated | Inference and validation private. [Listing](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/cal-ai-calorie-tracker/id6480417616) |
| MyFitnessPal | Food/exercise logs, recipes, partner apps | Free + Premium/Premium+; barcode/Meal Scan paid in current help comparison | Crowdsourced records can vary. [Plans](https://support.myfitnesspal.com/hc/en-us/articles/34889191368077-The-difference-between-Free-Premium-and-Premium) |
| Cronometer | Basic advertises up to 95 nutrients, >1M foods, integrations and unlimited barcode scanner | Basic $0; Gold paid | Database breadth does not ensure Indian cooked recipe coverage. [Basic](https://cronometer.com/features/free-nutrition-tracking-app.html) |

| Lose It! | Basic calorie budget and food DB; Premium has barcode/photo/voice features | Current help lists $79.99/year standard Premium; offers vary | Subscription gates and dynamic promotions. [Plans](https://loseit.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/51906523474708-Lose-It-Membership-Tiers-Pricing) |

| BiteSnap | Public site markets photo food journal | Current price/support not verified | Old marketing is insufficient to verify active capability. [Site](https://bite-snap.com/) |

## Findings
**Parity worth implementing:** quick logging, searchable foods, recents/favorites, meal templates, portions, barcode lookup, history, nutrient totals, export, editable photo suggestions.
**Differentiators:** regional aliases/transliteration, recipe-based home-food logging, local custom foods, provenance per nutrient, visible uncertainty, no shame/streak mechanics, full offline entry.
**Avoid:** proprietary coaching/cloud AI without free, private, optional runtime.

A systematic review found widely varying methods and calorie relative errors of roughly 0.1%–38.3%, preventing pooled synthesis ([review](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC10836267/)). App-store ratings and forum posts indicate friction, not accuracy. Photo error needs separate identity/portion/nutrient evaluation. Cite official product pages in table. Accessed 2026-10-11.
